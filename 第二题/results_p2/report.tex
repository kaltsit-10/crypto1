\section{Successful Solution: B\&B GSO Pruning}

\subsection{Preprocessing}
\textbf{Lattice basis} (BKZ-80, $200\times 200$):
$B = \bigl[\begin{smallmatrix} I_{100} & -A^T \\ 0 & q I_{100} \end{smallmatrix}\bigr]$
(3h23m, fpylll, strategies=default.json).

\textbf{Babai CVP} gave initial $\|v\|_\infty=24$, $\|u\|_\infty=19$.

\subsection{Algorithm}

GSO vectors $b_1^*,\ldots,b_{200}^*$ with $r_j=\|b_j^*\|^2$.
Babai coefficients: $c_j = \lfloor\langle t_{\text{curr}}, b_j^*\rangle / r_j\rceil$ ($j=199\to0$).

\textbf{Search}: last $K=8$ coefficients ($j=192\ldots199$), each $\pm4$ around Babai value.
Combinations: $9^8 \approx 4.3\times 10^7$.

\textbf{Pruning}: at level $j$, $\ell_2$ lower bound from dims $0\ldots j-1$:
$\ell_2^{\text{lb}} = \sqrt{\sum_{k=0}^{j-1} r_k \cdot \fracpart(\langle t_{\text{res}}, b_k^*\rangle / r_k)^2}$.
Prune if $\ell_2^{\text{lb}} > \ell_2^{\text{best}}$.
Dims $0\ldots191$ use Babai rounding only.

\subsection{Result}
\begin{center}
24 $\rightarrow$ 23 $\rightarrow$ 22 $\rightarrow$ 20 $\rightarrow$ 18 $\rightarrow$ 17 $\rightarrow$ 16 $\rightarrow$ \textbf{15}
\end{center}

\textbf{6 minutes}, 43M combinations. \\
$\|v\|_\infty=15$, $\|u\|_\infty=15$, $Av+u\equiv t\;(100)$ \checkmark\;
Solution: \texttt{results\_p2/solution\_p2\_20260521\_220739.txt}.

\section{Why B\&B Was Orders of Magnitude Faster}

\subsection{Right Subspace}
BKZ-80 GSO norm profile:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Dims 0--191: $r_j$ large ($\sim 10^4$), Babai rounding is accurate.
  \item Dims 192--199: $r_j$ small but $\mu_{j,k}$ propagate corrections to all 200 coordinates.
\end{itemize}
The Babai error lives almost entirely in the last 8 GSO dimensions.

\subsection{Right Pruning Metric}
$\ell_\infty$ pruning kills all branches at the root (partial $v$ have large $\ell_\infty$ from first rows).
$\ell_2$ bound accurately reflects remaining improvement room on well-reduced bases.

\subsection{Core Insight}
The CVP solution is only $24\to15$ away from Babai in $\ell_\infty$. This gap is concentrated in the last few GSO dimensions, where small coefficient changes produce large global effects via the $\mu$ matrix. Eight dimensions $\pm4$ with GSO-guided pruning finds it in minutes.

\section{Conclusion}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{BKZ-80} (3.4h) --- essential preprocessing on correct SIS basis.
  \item \textbf{Babai CVP} --- baseline (score=24), locally optimal, small perturbations cannot improve.
  \item \textbf{B\&B GSO pruning} (6min) --- enumerates last 8 GSO coefficients $\pm4$, $\ell_2$ pruning, escapes local minimum to score=15.
\end{enumerate}
